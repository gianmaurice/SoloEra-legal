Privacy policy — Solo Era

Last updated: 18 August 2026.

The short version

Solo Era needs an account, and the account holds your data. Your journal entries, your photos, your progress and your answers to the setup questions are stored on our backend so they come back whole on a new phone. Your phone keeps a copy so the app works offline.

We do not sell anything about you, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and there is no advertising or analytics SDK in the app.

Who we are

Solo Era is made by Gian Hagmann, Switzerland. Contact: support@soloera.app.

What we store

WhatWhy
Your email addressTo sign you in. It is the only way we identify you.
Your nameOnly if you use Sign in with Apple and Apple chooses to send it, which happens once, the first time.
Your journal entries — the mission, the date, both anxiety ratings, and any note you wroteSo they come back on a new phone.
Your photos — the picture on a journal entry, downscaled before it leaves your phoneSo an entry comes back with its picture. Each account can read and write only its own photos; nobody else's account can see yours.
Your progress — XP, level, streak, and which missions you have completedSame.
Your answers to the setup questions, including where you heard about Solo EraTo understand who is using the app and how people find it.
Your own custom missionsSame.

What we never store

  • Any password. Solo Era has none. You sign in with Apple, or with a six-digit code we email you.
  • Your location, your contacts, your health data, or anything from other apps. The app never asks for any of it. The photo you attach to an entry is the one you choose; the app reads nothing else from your library.
  • Advertising identifiers. There are none in the app.

Where it is stored, and who can see it

On Supabase, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany. Supabase is our data processor and stores this on our behalf — the rows in a database and the photos in a private storage bucket.

Every row and every photo is locked to your account at the database level, so one account cannot read another's. Gian can technically access the database and the storage as its administrator, and does so only to keep the service running.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, which tells the app whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an identifier for your account and information about your subscription. We never see your payment details — those stay with Apple.

Email

The code that signs you in is sent by Resend on our behalf. They process your email address to deliver it.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. Delete your account and it goes — see below.

Deleting your account

Your account (top right) → Delete account, inside the app.

That deletes your account and everything stored with it: your journal, your photos, your progress, your setup answers and your email address. It cannot be undone. The copy on your phone is cleared at the same time.

Signing out

Signing out clears the copy on your phone. Nothing is lost — your account still holds it, and signing back in brings it back.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at support@soloera.app. Deleting is faster from inside the app.

Children

Solo Era is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, the app will say so before the change applies.
